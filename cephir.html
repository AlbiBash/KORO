Description

Cephir opens with a vibrant, pulsing, sweet-and-sour pear-apple note: an uplifting, sparkling fountain that brings back some of the best moments in life, with a feeling of unlimited happiness and a cool glass of sparkling champagne in hand.

Further, the composition develops, and the cinnamon berries together with croton and eucalyptus flower create a perfect wildflower meadow flooded by sunlight, with a light touch of bitterness.

The scent is light and transparent. It gives freedom and additional space in any situation: whatever happens around you, it is a mind-liberating elixir.

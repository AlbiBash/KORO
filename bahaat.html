Details

Bahaat is a dark, warm perfume built around a cacao tincture made in our London studio. Roasted coffee gives it an edge, patchouli gives it depth, and tonka and benzoin leave a soft, resinous trail on the skin.

It is the closest we have come to the smell of real dark chocolate being broken in half: bitter, a little sweet, never sugary.

‘a real natural dark chocolate tincture that I was looking for so long!’
A KORO customer

Perfumer’s notes

Bahaat began with a bar of single-origin chocolate and a question: can a perfume smell like cacao itself, not like dessert? The answer took a season of tinctures, left to macerate until the chocolate note turned from sweet to serious.

Top notes

  • Roasted Coffee Coffea arabicaDark and aromatic
  • Cacao Tincture Theobroma cacaoBitter and rich

Heart notes

  • Patchouli Pogostemon cablinEarthy and velvety
  • Tonka Bean Dipteryx odorataWarm and almondy

Base notes

  • Benzoin Styrax benzoinResinous and soft
Materials

Composed from essential oils, absolutes, natural isolates, CO2 extracts and tinctures made in our studio. Natural materials vary from batch to batch, so colour and nuance may shift slightly over time. Full ingredient list on the product page at koroperfumes.com.

Shipping & care

Each batch is made fresh. Please allow 3–5 working days for dispatch. Keep away from direct sunlight and heat; apply to pulse points and let it settle for a few minutes.

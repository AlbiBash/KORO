Details

Mimosa captures the first warm days of the year, when the trees come into bloom and the whole street smells of honey and powder.

A golden, soft floral: mimosa absolute at its heart, with a touch of honey and beeswax, resting on vanilla.

‘time when nature wakes up and oozy fragrance of Mimosa tree filling the air’

Perfumer’s notes

Mimosa flowers for only a few weeks. The scent is fleeting, green at the edges and sweet in the middle, so we built the perfume to hold on to that moment a little longer than the tree can.

Top notes

  • Bergamot Citrus bergamiaZesty and uplifting
  • Green Mandarin Citrus nobilisFresh and bright

Heart notes

  • Mimosa Absolute Acacia dealbataPowdery and honeyed
  • Honey Absolute MelGolden and sweet

Base notes

  • Beeswax Absolute Cera albaWarm and waxy
  • Vanilla Vanilla planifoliaSoft and creamy
Materials

Composed from essential oils, absolutes, natural isolates, CO2 extracts and tinctures made in our studio. Natural materials vary from batch to batch, so colour and nuance may shift slightly over time. Full ingredient list on the product page at koroperfumes.com.

Shipping & care

Each batch is made fresh. Please allow 3–5 working days for dispatch. Keep away from direct sunlight and heat; apply to pulse points and let it settle for a few minutes.

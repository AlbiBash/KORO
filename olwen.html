Description

Olwen exudes an early-spring bouquet. It brings the feeling of the beginning of life and of the vegetation season: a never-ending rainbow of tender, delicate, airy acacia and hyacinth, completed with exceptional, unforgettable poplar buds and green grass notes. Styrax adds depth, warmth and complexity to the composition.

An uplifting, inspiring perfume that bestows an emotion of pure happiness, purity and innocence. The air becomes full of spring aromas: new tree buds, flowers and soaked soil warmed by bright spring sunlight.

The bottle & the box

Our square bottle, newly dressed

We kept the heavy square bottle our perfumes have always lived in, with its thick walls and deep glass base. The label is new: a plain beige sticker folded over the bottle’s front corner. KORO is embossed on one side; on the other, a close detail of a drawing by Nadya Danylenko runs off the edge.

A box that keeps the light out

Natural perfume changes in sunlight. Every bottle arrives in a rigid kraft box with a lift-off lid, made to live on your dressing table, so the perfume can go back into the dark between wears.

  • BoardOpaque rigid kraft that keeps daylight off the perfume
  • PrintKORO blind-embossed, with a detail of Nadya’s line in brown ink
  • FitA fitted base holds the square bottle upright
  • LidLifts off and settles back with a soft close
  • MaterialPaper and board only, nothing plastic
